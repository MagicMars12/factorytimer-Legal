1What this covers

This policy covers the Factory Timer mod and the Factory Timer Discord bot, together with the server that connects them. By using either one, you agree to what is described here.

2Information we collect

When you use the mod, the following is sent to and stored on our server:

  • Your Minecraft username, as the mod reports it. We do not verify it.
  • A random ID made for your install, and a scrambled (hashed) copy of a secret your mod generates. The secret itself is never stored. It lets the server recognise the same install next time. We do not store your Minecraft UUID, your email address or your real name.
  • Group information: group names, who is in each group and who owns it, invite codes and Discord link codes.
  • Factory information: the factory's type, the name you give it, its coordinates, its health, and the times it will stop and break.

When you use the Discord bot, we also store:

  • The ID of the Discord server and channel a group is connected to, and the IDs of the cards the bot posted there.
  • Command settings chosen by server admins, which can include Discord role or user IDs that are allowed to use a command.

The bot does not read messages in your server. It only answers slash commands, and it only posts in channels where a server admin connected it.

3How we use it

Only to run the service: to show your group's factories to its members, to count down timers, and to post and refresh cards in a Discord channel. We do not sell data, show ads, track you across other sites or use the data for anything else.

4Who can see it

  • Members of your group can see its factories, including their coordinates, and the names of the other members.
  • Other groups cannot see your group's factories.
  • Anyone who can read the Discord channel a group is connected to can see its cards.
  • The person running the service can access the database, as with any online service.

5Services we rely on

The service runs on Cloudflare (hosting and database) and posts through Discord. As with any web host, Cloudflare processes technical information such as IP addresses when the mod or the bot connects. These services handle data under their own policies.

6How long we keep it

Data is kept until the group is deleted, you leave the group, or you ask us to remove it. Messages posted in a Discord server are stored by Discord, and that server's admins can delete them.

7Your choices and data deletion

You can leave or delete a group at any time in the menu (.f). You can remove a factory with .f remove. To have your username and related data deleted, contact us: elitegroup897@gmail.com

8Children

The service is not meant for anyone under the minimum age to use Discord in their country, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

9Changes and contact

We may update this page and will change the date at the top. Questions about this policy: elitegroup897@gmail.com
